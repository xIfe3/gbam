Orders get lost in WhatsApp because chats are built for conversation, not for lists. A new order lands between a voice note and a "good morning", and by evening it is gone.

## Give every order one status

At any moment an order is in one of a few places: asked for, waiting for payment, paid, on its way, done. If you can say which one, you can't lose it. If you cannot, it is probably slipping.

## Look at one list, once or twice a day

Pick two moments, say mid-morning and evening. Go through every open order and move it on. Avoid scrolling chats to find out what is pending, because that is how things get missed.

## Handle the oldest first

Orders waiting for payment the longest are the ones most likely to go cold. A friendly nudge after a day or two costs you nothing.

## Close the loop with the customer

When an order is delivered, mark it done and say thanks. A tidy list at the end of the week tells you what sold, and what never got paid.

## Notebooks work too

A notebook is better than nothing. The trouble starts when you have two notebooks, a chat and a memory. Whatever you use, keep orders in one place.
