"How much?" "Is it available?" "What size?" If you answer these in chat all day, put the answers where customers can see them before they message.

## The five details

1. **Name that says what it is.** "Ankara tote bag, medium" beats "New arrival".
2. **Price in naira, final.** If delivery is extra, say so in the description rather than surprising people later.
3. **Size, colour or quantity options.** Write exactly what you have, in the words customers use.
4. **What is in the box or order.** One line is enough.
5. **How long it takes.** Say when you can pack, or how long made-to-order items take.

## Keep it short

A listing is not an essay. Two or three plain sentences are easier to read on a phone than a long paragraph, and customers read on phones.

## Check it like a customer

Open your own shop link on your phone, as if you had never seen the product. Is anything still a question? Add that detail.

## Keep prices honest

If a price changes, change it in your shop right away. Customers who order at one price and are asked for another stop trusting you quickly.
