You can refund any order the customer has paid for, including one you cancelled after they paid.

## Steps

1. Open the order in your dashboard.
2. At the bottom, find **Refund the customer**. It shows how much they paid and the Paystack payment reference. Use **Copy reference** to copy it.
3. Give the money back, in one of two ways:
   - **Quickest:** send it back to the customer yourself by bank transfer.
   - **Or** email **support@gbam.app** with the payment reference. We reverse the card payment through Paystack. It can take a few working days to reach the customer.
4. Tick **The customer has their money back**.
5. Press **Mark refunded**.

The customer gets a WhatsApp message, and their tracking page shows the order as refunded.

## Good to know

- Marking an order refunded can't be undone, so only do it once the money is back with the customer.
- You can't refund an order that was never paid. Cancel it instead.
