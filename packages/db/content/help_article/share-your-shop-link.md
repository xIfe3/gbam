Your shop link is `gbam.app/` followed by your shop name. Anyone who opens it sees your products and can place an order.

## Where to share it

- Paste it in your WhatsApp status and your WhatsApp Business profile.
- Put it in your Instagram or TikTok bio.
- Send it in chats when someone asks "how much?" or "what do you have?".

## What the customer does

1. They open your link and choose what they want.
2. They enter their name and WhatsApp number.
3. They place the order.

The order shows up in your dashboard as **New**. Nothing is paid yet. You decide the next step, which is to [request payment](/help/request-payment).
