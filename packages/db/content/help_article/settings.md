Open **Settings** from the tabs at the top of your dashboard.

## Shop

Change your **shop name** or **WhatsApp number** and press **Save**. Your shop link stays the same, so links you have already shared keep working. A WhatsApp number can only belong to one shop.

## Getting paid

Shows the bank account your payouts go to (only the last 4 digits). To switch it, choose **Change bank account**. See [how you get paid](/help/how-you-get-paid).

## Password

Type your current password and a new one (at least 8 characters). Any other phones or computers signed in to your account are signed out. The one you are using stays signed in.

## Delete my account

See [delete your account](/help/delete-your-account).
