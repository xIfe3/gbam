Customers don't need an account. After ordering, they get everything they need to follow their order.

## After they order

The order page shows their **order code** (like `GB-7K3Q`) and a private link to their order, which they can copy. If they give an email address, we also send a receipt with the code and the link. Orders placed on a phone are remembered on that phone.

## Tracking page

The link opens a page showing where the order is (received, waiting for payment, paid, sent out, delivered), what they ordered and the total. Anyone without the link can go to **gbam.app/track** and enter the order code and the last 4 digits of their phone number.

## Paying

When you request payment, the customer gets a WhatsApp link to a **Review & pay** page. It shows their items and total, then opens Paystack's secure payment window. They can also pay from the tracking page while the order is waiting for payment.

## Getting help

The tracking page has a button to message you on WhatsApp, and a **Report a problem** form (for example, "paid but not marked paid" or "haven't received my order"). Reported problems show on the order in your dashboard, and we email you about them.
