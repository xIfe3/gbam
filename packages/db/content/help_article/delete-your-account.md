There is no delete-account button. To delete your account, email **privacy@gbam.app** from the email address on your account and say you want it deleted.

## What happens

We will confirm it is you, then handle your request as described on the [Data Deletion page](/legal/data-deletion), which explains what is removed and what we may need to keep.

## Before you ask

- Finish or cancel any orders in progress.
- Save anything you need, such as your order history.
