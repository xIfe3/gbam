You can delete your account yourself in **Settings**.

## Steps

1. In your dashboard, open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Delete my account**.
3. Type your shop name exactly as it appears, and your password.
4. Press **Delete my account**.

## What happens

- Your shop disappears straight away. Your shop link stops working and nobody can place new orders or pay for open ones.
- You are signed out on every phone and computer, and you can't sign back in.
- We are told to delete your data and handle it as described on the [Data Deletion page](/legal/data-deletion), which explains what is removed and what we may need to keep.

This can't be undone from your dashboard. If you change your mind, email **support@gbam.app** as soon as you can.

## Before you delete

- Finish, cancel or refund any orders in progress. Customers can't pay a closed shop.
- Save anything you need, such as your order history.
