Your Gbam shop is a page customers can order from. It has its own link, `gbam.app/` followed by your shop name.

## Set it up

1. Go to **Sign up** and enter your email and a password.
2. Choose a shop name. This becomes your link.
3. Add your bank account details so your payouts have somewhere to go. See [how you get paid](/help/how-you-get-paid).
4. Add your products, each with a name and a price. A short description helps customers choose.

## Before you share it

Open your shop link yourself and check that your names and prices look right. You can change products in your dashboard at any time.

Next step: [share your shop link](/help/share-your-shop-link).
