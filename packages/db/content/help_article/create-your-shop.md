Your Gbam shop is a page customers can order from. It has its own link, `gbam.app/` followed by your shop name.

## Set it up

1. Go to **Sign up** and enter your shop name. We show the link it gives you, `gbam.app/` followed by your shop name, and check it is free.
2. Enter your WhatsApp number, your email address and a password, then choose **Continue**.
3. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it to verify your email address.
4. Add your bank details so your payouts have somewhere to go. See [how you get paid](/help/how-you-get-paid). You can choose **Skip for now** and add them later, but you can't send payment links until you do.
5. You land on your dashboard. Your shop is ready.

## Add your products

Product editing in your dashboard is coming soon. Until then, email support@gbam.app with your products and prices and we will add them for you.

## Before you share it

Open your shop link yourself and check that your names and prices look right. To change a product or price for now, email support@gbam.app.
