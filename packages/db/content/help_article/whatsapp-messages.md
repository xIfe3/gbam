Gbam sends your customer WhatsApp messages for you as an order moves along, so you do not have to type each update.

## When messages go out

- When you request payment, with a link to pay for the order.
- At the later steps of the order, such as when payment is confirmed and when the order moves forward.

## Where they come from

Messages are sent through Gbam's WhatsApp business number, not your personal number. Customers can still chat with you on your own WhatsApp as usual.

## Seeing what was sent

Open an order in your dashboard to see its messages and their delivery status: accepted, sent, delivered, read or failed. If a message failed, the reason is shown, so you can message the customer yourself.

## The customer's number matters

Messages go to the WhatsApp number the customer typed when ordering. If it is wrong or not on WhatsApp, the message will fail.
