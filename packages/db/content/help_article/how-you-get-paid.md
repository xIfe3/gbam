Customers pay through Paystack. Your share goes to the bank account you added when you signed up, through a Paystack subaccount that is set up for you.

## What comes out

- Paystack charges its own processing fee on each payment.
- Gbam charges a small fee on each paid order. We only charge when an order is paid. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the current rate and an example calculator.

You receive what is left. Your order page shows the fees for each payment.

## When the money arrives

Paystack, not Gbam, pays you out: your share reaches your bank account the next working day after the customer pays (a payment on Friday evening or the weekend arrives on Monday). While Paystack is still verifying an account, it can hold payouts until that's done. If a payout looks late, email support@gbam.app with the order code and we will help you check.

## Changing your bank account

Open **Settings**, then **Getting paid**, and choose **Change bank account**. We check the new account with your bank, show you its name, and ask for your password before switching. New payments go to the new account. Payments you have already received are not affected.

We email you whenever your bank account is changed. If you didn't make the change, email **support@gbam.app** straight away and change your password.
