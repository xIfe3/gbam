---
title: How do customers pay?
category: Payments
sortOrder: 3
published: true
date: 2026-10-07
---

When you request payment on an order, the customer gets a link on WhatsApp. It opens a page showing what they ordered and the total, and a **Pay** button that opens Paystack's secure payment window. Card details go straight to Paystack, so neither you nor Gbam sees them. When Paystack confirms the payment, the order becomes Paid automatically.
