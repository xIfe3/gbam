---
title: Can I cancel an order?
category: Orders
sortOrder: 10
published: true
date: 2026-10-07
---

Yes, any time before the customer pays. Open the order and press **Cancel order…** at the bottom of the page, then confirm. You then have 5 seconds to press **Undo** before the order is cancelled and the customer gets a WhatsApp message.

Once an order is Paid it can no longer be cancelled, only refunded. See [can I refund an order?](/faq).
