---
title: Can I refund an order?
category: Orders
sortOrder: 9
published: true
date: 2026-10-07
---

Yes. Open the order and use **Refund the customer** at the bottom of the page. It shows how much they paid and the Paystack payment reference.

Give the money back first, in one of two ways:

- send it back to the customer yourself by bank transfer, or
- email **support@gbam.app** with the payment reference and we reverse the card payment through Paystack (it can take a few working days to reach the customer).

Then tick **The customer has their money back** and press **Mark refunded**. The customer gets a WhatsApp message. You can also refund an order you cancelled, if the customer had already paid for it. See [refund an order](/help/refund-an-order).
