- Add products yourself from **Products**: photos, name, description, price, and options such as size or colour, each with its own price and stock.
- Hide a product without deleting it.
- Customers pick options and build a basket across product pages.
- Stock comes off when an order is paid, and you are warned if something sold out at the same time.
