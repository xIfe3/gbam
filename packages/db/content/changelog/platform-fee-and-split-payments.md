---
title: Platform fee and split payments
summary: Payments are split automatically, with Gbam's fee taken per paid order.
sortOrder: 3
published: true
date: 2026-10-03
---

When a customer pays, Paystack now splits the payment: Gbam's fee on that paid order, and the rest to the seller's bank account through a Paystack subaccount. The fees for each payment are recorded on the order. See [Pricing](/pricing) for the rate.
