- **Settings**: change your shop name and WhatsApp number, switch the bank account your payouts go to (we check it with your bank and ask for your password), change your password, or delete your account.
- **Refunds**: each paid order has a **Refund the customer** section with the payment reference and the steps to give the money back. Orders you cancelled after the customer paid can be refunded too.
- **Cancel with Undo**: cancelling now asks you to confirm, then gives you 5 seconds to undo before the customer is told.
- We email you whenever your payout bank account is changed.
