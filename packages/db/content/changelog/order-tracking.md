---
title: Order tracking for customers
summary: Customers can follow their order, pay and get help without an account.
sortOrder: 3
published: true
date: 2026-10-06
---

- After ordering, customers get an order code and a private link to their order, plus an email receipt if they give an email address.
- The tracking page shows where the order is, what they ordered and the total, with a button to pay while payment is waiting.
- Anyone can find an order at gbam.app/track with the code and the last 4 digits of their phone number.
- Customers can report a problem from the tracking page. You see it on the order and get an email.
