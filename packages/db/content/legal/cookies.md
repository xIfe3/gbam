  - id: the-cookie
    heading: The cookies we use
    body: |
      - **`gbam_session`**: keeps a seller logged in to their account. It is an essential cookie.
      - **`gbam_admin`**: keeps a member of the Gbam team signed in to the admin area. Only Gbam staff ever get it, and it lasts at most 12 hours. It is an essential cookie.

      Each is set when someone signs in and removed when they sign out or it expires. They are needed for the dashboard and the admin area to work.
    short: Only gbam_session (sellers) and gbam_admin (Gbam staff), which keep people signed in.
  - id: what-we-dont-use
    heading: What we do not use
    body: |
      We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking cookies. Because we only use an essential cookie, we do not show a cookie banner.

      If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.
    short: No analytics or ad cookies, so no cookie banner.
  - id: your-choices
    heading: Your choices
    body: |
      You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block `gbam_session`, you will not be able to stay logged in to the seller dashboard. Customers ordering from a shop do not need an account or this cookie.
    short: You can block cookies, but sellers then cannot log in.
