sections:
  - id: seller-to-customer
    heading: Refunds are between seller and customer
    body: |
      When you order from a Gbam shop, the sale is with the seller. If you want a refund, ask the seller. Each seller decides their own return and refund terms and should state them in their shop or on WhatsApp.

      Gbam is operated by **Zephra Studios (registration pending)**, RC number **[RC number]**, of **[Registered address]**. We are not the seller and we cannot decide a seller's refund.
    short: Ask the seller. They decide and pay refunds.
  - id: no-automatic-refunds
    heading: Gbam does not process refunds automatically
    body: |
      Gbam does not send money back to customers. If a seller marks an order **Refunded** in the dashboard, that records the status only. The seller must actually refund the customer directly, for example by bank transfer.
    short: Marking an order "Refunded" is a record. It does not send any money.
  - id: gbam-fee
    heading: What happens to Gbam's fee
    body: |
      The Gbam fee on a refunded order is not automatically returned to the seller. If you are a seller and think a fee should be reviewed, email support@gbam.app with the order details and we will look at it. See the [Pricing page](/pricing) for how fees work.

      Paystack's own processing fees follow Paystack's terms. Gbam does not control them.
    short: Gbam's fee is not automatically returned. Email support@gbam.app to ask for a review.
  - id: customers-stuck
    heading: If the seller does not respond
    body: |
      Contact the seller first. If you cannot reach them, email support@gbam.app with your order details. We can pass your message on and, where a seller is breaking our rules, take action on the account, but we cannot promise a refund.

      If you paid by card, you may also have rights through your bank or card issuer.
    short: We can pass on your message but cannot promise a refund.
