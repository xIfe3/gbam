  - id: what-we-store
    heading: What we store
    body: |
      **Sellers**

      - Account details: email address and a hashed password (we cannot read your password). We do not ask for your personal name.
      - Shop details: business name, shop link, WhatsApp number, products and prices.
      - Payout details: bank name, the account holder name and the **last 4 digits** of your account number only. The full account number is held by Paystack, not by us.
      - Login sessions: the IP address and a description of the browser or device for each signed-in session, for security and fraud prevention.

      **Customers who order from a shop**

      - Name, WhatsApp number and order details (what was ordered, and any delivery information the seller asks for).

      **Payments**

      - Payment status and fees for each order.
      - From Paystack's payment record we keep the card type, the first 6 and last 4 digits of the card, the expiry month and year, the issuing bank, the payer's IP address and the customer details Paystack returns.

      **Messages**

      - WhatsApp message delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed).
      - Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number and your message. To limit spam we briefly use your IP address in a short-lived counter (for up to an hour); it is not saved with your message.

      **We never see or store full card numbers or CVVs.** Card details are entered with Paystack, which shares only the partial card details above with us.
    short: Only what we need to run orders. Never full card numbers or CVVs, but Paystack shares partial card details with us for each payment.
  - id: why
    heading: Why we use it
    body: |
      - To run your account and shop, take orders and request payments.
      - To send order messages and updates to customers on WhatsApp.
      - To send sellers email for verification codes and password resets, and to reply to contact-form messages.
      - To keep Gbam secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
      - To answer questions you send us.
      - To meet legal and accounting obligations.

      Our legal bases under the NDPA are performing our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a safe service, compliance with the law, and your consent where we ask for it.
    short: We use data to run orders, send updates, stay secure and obey the law.
  - id: who-we-share-with
    heading: Who we share it with
    body: |
      We use these processors to provide Gbam:

      - **Paystack** for payments.
      - **Meta (WhatsApp)** to deliver order messages.
      - **Resend** to send email.
      - **Our hosting provider** to run and store the service.

      A seller can see their own customers' names, WhatsApp numbers and order details. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data where the law or a court requires it.

      Some of these providers process data outside Nigeria. We only share what each one needs to do its job.
    short: Paystack, Meta/WhatsApp, Resend and our hosting provider. We do not sell your data.
  - id: retention
    heading: How long we keep it
    body: |
      We keep account data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account we complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming the request came from the account owner (see [Data Deletion](/legal/data-deletion)). We may keep some records for longer where the law, tax or accounting rules, or Paystack's requirements say we must, or where we need them to handle a dispute. Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to answer and follow up.
    short: We keep data while your account is open, then delete it within 30 days of confirming the request is from you, apart from records we must keep.
  - id: your-rights
    heading: Your rights
    body: |
      Under the NDPA you have the right to:

      - **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
      - **Correct** data that is wrong or incomplete.
      - **Erase** your data, in the cases the law provides.
      - **Restrict** how we use your data.
      - **Object** to certain uses, including where we rely on legitimate interests.
      - **Port** your data to you or another service in a common format.
      - **Withdraw consent** at any time where we rely on it.
      - **Complain** to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

      To use any right, email privacy@gbam.app from the email on your account. If you are a customer of a seller, tell us the WhatsApp number you used. We may need to confirm who you are first.
    short: You can see, fix, delete, restrict, object to or move your data, and complain to the NDPC.
  - id: security
    heading: Security
    body: |
      We protect data with measures such as hashed passwords and access controls, and we limit who can see it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that data can never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.
    short: We take sensible precautions, but nothing online is risk-free.

    short: One login cookie, 18+ only, and we will update the date when this changes.
