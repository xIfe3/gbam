  - id: parties
    heading: This agreement
    body: |
      This agreement is between you (the seller) and **Zephra Studios (registration pending)**, RC number **[RC number]**, of **[Registered address]**. It applies together with the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).
    short: This is the extra set of rules for people who sell on Gbam.
  - id: your-shop
    heading: Your shop and listings
    body: |
      You are responsible for your shop, listings, prices, photos and descriptions. They must be accurate and you must have the right to sell what you list. You must be at least 18, give accurate account details, and keep them up to date.
    short: You are responsible for what you list being true and lawful.
  - id: orders
    heading: Fulfilling orders
    body: |
      When a customer pays, you are responsible for delivering what they ordered, on time and as described, and for dealing with questions and complaints. Gbam is not the seller and does not ship goods.
    short: Delivering the order and looking after the customer is on you.
  - id: fees
    heading: Fees and payouts
    body: |
      We charge a Gbam fee on each paid order and Paystack charges its processing fee, which comes out of your share. Current fees are on the [Pricing page](/pricing). Payments are processed and paid out by Paystack, and you agree to Paystack's terms as they apply to you. We are not responsible for delays or holds on Paystack's side.
    short: Gbam's fee and Paystack's fee come out of each paid order.
  - id: refunds
    heading: Refunds
    body: |
      You decide your own refund terms and must refund customers directly. Marking an order "Refunded" only records the status. Gbam's fee is not automatically returned. See the [Refunds Policy](/legal/refunds).
    short: You refund customers yourself. "Refunded" is only a status.
  - id: customer-data
    heading: Your customers' data
    body: |
      You will see customers' names, WhatsApp numbers and order details. Use them only to fulfil and support their orders and as the law allows. Do not sell them, spam customers, or share them without a lawful reason. You are responsible for how you use data once you have it, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
    short: Use customer details only to serve their orders.
  - id: whatsapp
    heading: WhatsApp messages
    body: |
      Gbam sends order messages through WhatsApp on your behalf. Messages must follow WhatsApp's and Meta's rules. We may limit or stop messages if they are being misused.
    short: Order messages must follow WhatsApp's rules.
  - id: ending
    heading: Suspension and closing
    body: |
      We may suspend or close a shop that breaks these rules, puts customers at risk, or is subject to a legal order. You can close your account at any time by following the steps in [Data Deletion](/legal/data-deletion). Please finish and settle open orders first.
    short: We can close shops that break the rules. You can leave anytime.
