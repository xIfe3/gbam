    heading: What you may not sell
    body: |
      You may not list or sell:

      - Anything illegal in Nigeria, or that you have no right to sell.
      - Counterfeit or stolen goods.
      - Illegal drugs, weapons or ammunition.
      - Fake, unregistered or expired medicine, food or cosmetics.
      - Adult sexual content or services, or anything involving the exploitation of children.
      - Human beings, human organs, or protected wildlife.
      - Fake documents, IDs or certificates.
      - Anything that is a scam, a pyramid scheme or a fake giveaway.
    short: Nothing illegal, fake, stolen or harmful.
  - id: prohibited-conduct
    heading: What you may not do
    body: |
      - Mislead customers about a product, a price or delivery.
      - Take payment for goods you do not intend to deliver.
      - Harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
      - Send spam or unwanted messages, or misuse customers' phone numbers.
      - Try to break, overload or gain access to parts of Gbam that are not yours, or use it to commit fraud or launder money.
      - Pretend to be another person or business.
    short: No fraud, spam, harassment or hacking.
  - id: enforcement
    heading: What happens if you break the rules
    body: |

    short: We can remove listings and close accounts, and report serious cases.
