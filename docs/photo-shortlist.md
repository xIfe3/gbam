# Photo shortlist

One row per `<Photo description="...">` used on the site. Later tasks append their rows.
Nothing is downloaded until the owner approves a photo; approved photos go into
`apps/web/public/photos/`.

Licences: **Unsplash License** and **Pexels License** both allow free commercial use without
attribution. Links marked "search" are result pages to pick a specific photo from. Every link
below was returned by a web search; before approving, open it and check the licence label under
the photo (avoid anything marked "Unsplash+", which is paid). Prefer photos of Black African people
and Nigerian settings where possible.
