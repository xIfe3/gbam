C · Summary cards + collapsible sections

Green header, three "at a glance" cards (collect / never / you can), then sections that open and close. Friendliest and fastest to skim. Collapsed text is a bit less transparent for a legal page.

My suggestion: B, perhaps with C's three "at a glance" cards at the top. Long legal docs (Terms, Seller Agreement) really benefit from "jump to section", and per-section short versions build trust.
