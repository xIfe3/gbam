D · "g" monogram + coral dot

A soft green tile with a serif "g" and a coral notification dot ("you have a new order"). Clean app-icon feel, scales perfectly. Less storytelling than B or C.

My pick: C if you want Gbam to be memorable and fun, D if you want it to scale cleanly everywhere. A good option is C's idea, simplified: fewer burst points so it survives 16px. Whatever you pick, a designer can refine it later; this sets the direction.
